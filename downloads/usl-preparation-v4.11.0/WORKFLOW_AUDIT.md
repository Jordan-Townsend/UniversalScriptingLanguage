The intended growth path is evidence-based: a new learned skill or policy first passes independent contracts, new one-use heldouts when learning, and existing-skill retention. Only then may it take over a real registered operation. Policy/source/dependency changes invalidate relevant evidence. Metadata or source-text similarity does not qualify semantics. Native observations and external effects are never replayed.

In the latest run, all10,926 logical actors started together,60 ID qualifications were coalesced into32 process jobs, and every required check passed.6534 ID/dimension actors remain explicitly tied to missing implementations (363IDs×18);3294 refer to prior scope;1080 have fresh bounded-profile evidence;18 refer to the extra USL base. Full normative dimensions remain open in every group.

This audit does not promise arbitrary natural-language intent proofs, complete compiler libraries/ABIs, autonomous invention of missing language semantics, human senses or general AI self-study. Those remain engineering and verification tracks.
